# AgOpenWeb user guides

Looking for the one-page list of everything AgOpenWeb adds? See
[What AgOpenWeb does beyond AgOpenGPS](../FEATURES.md).

Plain-language guides for things AgOpenWeb does beyond stock AgOpenGPS.
Written for the person in the tractor seat, not for programmers.

| Guide | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Turning at the headland](turns.md) | Automatic U and K turns: the countdown readout, swapping direction, the reverse-leg K turn, snake skip rows, and hard fences that refuse a turn the implement won't clear |
| [Offset implements](tool-offset.md) | Implements that sit to one side of the tractor: the one setting, what the guidance line does, alternating U-turns, and planning with offset |
| [Route Planner basics](route-planner-basics.md) | A short tour of the Route Planner: patterns, split lines, planning, steering the plan, and the ETA |
| [Route Planner — beyond the basics](route-planner-advanced.md) | Automatic pass angle, cross-drill and weave, split blocks, planning along a track, obstacles and ponds, pause and resume, slope-corrected spacing |
| [Headland styles](headland-styles.md) | The Route Planner's Headland tab: Classic laps, Spiral in, Spiral out, None — and which one suits which job |
| [Building a field](field-builder.md) | Recording or drawing a boundary, Drive Thru and Hard flags on inner boundaries, headland, flags, the Quick AB Line dialog and the boundary-curve trim buttons |
| [Fields, jobs and coverage records](fields-jobs-coverage.md) | Permanent paddocks with one job per piece of work, product and applied total, cross-drill coverage channels, and the export that feeds the farm-server viewer |
| [Terrain](terrain-map.md) | The field elevation map: recorded automatically while you drive, shown with the Terrain toggle, and what altitude accuracy to expect |
| [Tank mix calculator](tank-mix.md) | Spray mix arithmetic: water, fills, per-tank chemical amounts, refill points on the route |
| [Setting up rate control](rate-control-setup.md) | The one-off setup: turning rate control on for a tool, products and channels, commissioning and tuning a module from the cab, catch-test calibration, switches and primed start |
| [Rate control: switchbox, readout and alarms](rate-switchbox.md) | The floating switchbox and rate readout, the physical AOG_RC box, Network IO rate rows, and the drop-off alarm |
| [Connecting your modules](module-connections.md) | Getting steer, machine and rate boards talking to AgOpenWeb — Ethernet, WiFi or USB — and what to check when a module won't show up |
| [The work switch](work-switch.md) | Maintained switch vs momentary button, wiring polarity, what the switch actually turns on and off, and how to bench-test it |
| [Other devices and who is driving](remote-and-seats.md) | Opening the app on a phone or tablet, the Operator / Observer seat, what an Observer can't touch, the lost-operator safety stop, and the full layout on a small screen |
| [Your data and profiles](data-and-profiles.md) | Where the data folder is and what is in it, vehicle profile vs tool profile, importing from AgOpenGPS, and backing up without letting cloud sync eat your files |

All screenshots of button and tab names in these guides match the app as built.
If a name here doesn't match your screen, you are probably on an older version.
