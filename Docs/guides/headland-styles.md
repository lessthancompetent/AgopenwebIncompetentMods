# Headland styles

**What this is.** When the Route Planner builds a route, it can work the
headland as laps around the paddock as well as filling the middle. The
**Headland** tab lets you choose *how* those laps are driven and *when* —
before or after the middle. Pick once; the choice is remembered on this device.

**You will need**

- A field with a boundary
- The Route Planner: left bar → **Field Tools** → **Route Planner** →
  **Headland** tab

## The controls

**Headland laps** — how many laps around the outside. Leave it on **Auto** to
let the planner size it from your U-turn radius, or step it up/down.

**Style** — how the laps are driven:

| Style | What it does | Pick it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Classic laps** | Separate closed laps, outermost first — the familiar way. Stop, step in one width, go again. | You want what you're used to, or you lift out at the end of each lap anyway. |
| **Spiral in** | One continuous path from the fence inward. Each lap closes at its own start, then a smooth lane change moves you one width in — no stop-start between laps. | Long headlands where the stop at every lap join wastes time (spraying, spreading, rolling). |
| **Spiral out** | The same spiral driven inside-out, finishing on the fence lap. | You want to end the job at the gate — pair it with **Headland last**. |
| **None** | No headland laps in this route at all. Turning room is still kept clear. | You'll work the headland separately or by hand (e.g. someone else mows it). |

**Order** — when the laps happen:

- **Headland first** — laps, then the middle. Your U-turns then run on ground
  that is already worked. Right for **spraying and seeding**: you never turn on
  unsprayed/unsown ground.
- **Headland last** — middle first, laps on the way out. The headland stays
  clean for turning and is worked last. Right for **mowing**: you don't drive
  over cut grass all day, and the flattened turning ground gets cut at the end.

**Back-cut finish lap** — adds one extra fence-tight lap at the very end,
driven the *opposite* way round. For one-sided tools (disc mowers, side-delivery
rakes) this puts the working side against the fence, so the last strip along
the fence line gets properly dressed. Leave it off for symmetrical tools.

## Notes

- The hint line under the buttons repeats what the selected style will do.
- If you choose the whole-field **Spiral** pattern on the Pattern tab, the
  Headland controls disappear — that pattern is one spiral fence-to-centre and
  has no separate headland.
- Your choices (style, order, back-cut) are stored on the tablet or phone you
  set them on. The mower's tablet keeps "Spiral out + Headland last + Back-cut"
  while the sprayer's keeps "Headland first" — they don't fight.
- Nothing changes on the map until you tap **Plan Route** again.
